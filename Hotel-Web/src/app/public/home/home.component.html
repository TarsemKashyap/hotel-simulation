What you will get ?

The Hotel Simulation is a training tool designed to help participants develop critical skills in hotel business management. It focuses on problem identification, prioritization, and problem-solving, with an emphasis on understanding the cause-and-effect relationships between performance outcomes and the factors that drive them. The simulation also encourages teamwork, as participants work together as a hotel management team, each responsible for a specific operational area.

By participating in the simulation, team members learn to plan, analyze, and apply market research and information systems to support strategic decision-making. It helps them build the ability to assess business challenges, think analytically, and make data-driven recommendations.

The Hotel Simulation replicates real-world competitive market conditions, where multiple hotels compete in a virtual marketplace. Initially, all hotels are identical, but as management teams make decisions, the simulation adjusts demand and revenue based on those choices as well as the actions of competing hotels. Key operational decisions—such as capital investment, operating costs, marketing strategies, and pricing—are all factors that influence the outcome. The simulation also considers external elements like day-of-week patterns and fluctuating demand.

This risk-free environment allows participants to manage a hotel without financial consequences. After each round, results are shared with all teams, providing insights into how their actions, and the actions of their competitors, affect performance. The Hotel Simulation offers valuable training for managers and management trainees to improve their operational and leadership skills, while also providing an enriching educational experience for students in hospitality business management.

About Us

WELCOME TO HOTEL BUSINESS MANAGEMENT TRAINING SIMULATION!

The Hotel Business Management Training Simulation is a simulation used for hotel business management training and analysis. It is designed to help participants to develop problem identification, problem prioritizing, and problem solving skills; specifically the development of skills to understand and apply the cause and effect relationships between performance measures (effects) and their causes. It is also designed to enable participants to develop team building and teamwork skills by making participants act as a member of a hotel management team. Each member of the team acts as the manager of a specific operational area of a hotel. This simulation enables the members of each management team to develop the ability to plan, analyze and apply market research and information systems knowledge for the strategic business planning process. It provides the skills to analyze the business situation, analytically think about the situation, evaluate, and make recommendations based on available information.
